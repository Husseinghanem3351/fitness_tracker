====================================================
           ZELORA - GOOGLE PLAY STORE DETAILS
====================================================

1. APPLICATION IDENTIFIERS:
   - App Name: Zelora
   - Package Name (Application ID): com.zelora.app
   - Category: Health & Fitness
   - App Type: Free App

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION (Max 80 Characters):
   Track daily calories, meals, workouts, water, and fitness streaks with Zelo!

3. FULL DESCRIPTION:
   Zelora is your smart, interactive daily calorie and health companion!

   Key Features:
   • Smart Calorie Budgeting: Personalized BMR & TDEE target calorie calculations based on your health goals (weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance).
   • Interactive Fitness Pet (Zelo): Meet Zelo! Your personal fitness mascot that reacts dynamically to your progress, levels up (+XP), and maintains daily streaks (🔥).
   • Drag-and-Drop Quick Logger: Drag water, meals, or workouts directly onto Zelo to log them instantly!
   • Meal & Nutrition Library: Comprehensive meal database, custom recipe builder, and macronutrient tracking (Proteins, Carbs, Fats).
   • Exercise & Workout Tracker: Track calories burned during workouts or log custom activities.
   • Water Intake Tracker: Interactive water tracker with customizable daily hydration goals.
   • Local Reminders: Smart water and meal logging notifications to keep you on track.
   • Multi-Language Support: Full support for English and Arabic.

4. BUILD COMMANDS:
   - Generate Release App Bundle (.aab):
     flutter build appbundle --release

   - Generated File Location:
     build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab

5. GOOGLE PLAY TESTERS SETUP (Internal / Closed Testing):
   - In Google Play Console, go to: Release > Internal testing (or Closed testing).
   - Click the "Testers" tab.
   - Create an Email List (e.g. "Zelora Beta Testers") and add the Gmail addresses of your testers.
   - Copy the "Join on Android" / "Join on Web" opt-in link provided by Google Play and share it with your testers!
